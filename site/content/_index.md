---
title: godotnvim
tagline: Neovim as Godot's script editor
---

A Godot 4 editor plugin that edits GDScript, shaders and other text files in
an embedded Neovim, drawn inside the editor with Neovim's UI protocol. Your
config, plugins and keymaps load as usual, and the parts of Godot's script
editor that matter keep working.
